Planetary Gear-Based Mechanical Battery

Overview

Project Summary

Designed and built a fully mechanical energy storage device that captures 10 J from a falling 2 kg mass, stores it for 10 seconds, then releases it to lift a 1 kg output mass — with no electrical components. The chosen design, the Spring Cluster, uses a planetary gearbox and four 3D-printed PLA spiral torsion springs. It achieved a measured efficiency of 36%, placing 3rd in the cohort by efficiency-to-weight ratio.

Conclusion

Summary & Outcomes

The Spring Cluster battery met all fixed requirements, completed 3+ reliable cycles, and achieved 36% measured efficiency (vs. 54% theoretical). Key losses came from PLA gear/spring friction and assembly tolerances. Ranked 3rd in the cohort by efficiency-to-weight ratio.
