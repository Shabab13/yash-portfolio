Torsional spring 3D-printed PLA torsion coil springs
Efficiency Recovery 33% measured kinetic efficiency (against 55% theoretical limits)

Project Overview

Mechanical energy storage devices, like flywheels or springs, provide high power density and infinite charge-discharge cycles compared to chemical batteries. This project developed a compact, high-efficiency mechanical battery. It uses a planetary gear train speed multiplier connected to 3D printed torsion springs, storing potential energy that can be released as rotational kinetic force.

Key Deliverables

  • Designed involute gear tooth profiles in SolidWorks to guarantee smooth gear engagement without binding under spring load.
  • Programmed and executed toolpaths on a manual lathe to machine the steel AISI 4140 main shafts down to +/- 0.02 mm tolerances.
  • Assembled the planetary carrier housing with 5 planet gears to distribute tangential loads evenly across the gears.
  • Designed laser-cutter paths for the birch plywood casing structure, incorporating a clear acrylic viewport.
  • Analyzed the energy conversion path to isolate friction losses, finding major friction in the lower guide bearing assembly.
  • Documented recommendations to scale spring storage by switching from PLA to spring-steel coils.
